==================================================

SECONDARY
CONTEXTUAL

PRIMARY:

SECONDARY:
Less frequently used destinations shown inside "More" or the existing secondary navigation mechanism.

CONTEXTUAL:
Feature-specific destinations that should appear inside the relevant page/workspace and NOT in the global navigation.
